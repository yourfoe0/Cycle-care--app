cyclecare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

What we collect

  • Account info: display name, email and password.
  • Cycle data: period start dates, logged symptoms, cycle length history.
  • Pregnancy data (optional): due date or last-period date, used to estimate week and trimester.
  • Shared space data: checklist items, notes, love notes, and gestures sent between paired partners, plus your partner-pairing code and status-sharing preference.
  • Usage data: app opens, feature usage, crash logs.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or camera/microphone data.

How data is shared

  • Cycle log data is private by default and never shown to a partner unless the "Share my status" toggle is turned on.
  • Shared-space content is visible only to the specific partner you've paired with via invite code.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not show ads inside the app.
  • Data may be shared with service providers strictly to operate the app, under contracts that prohibit other use.

Where data lives & security

  • Account and app data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL), encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Database access rules restrict each account to its own data and its paired partner's shared data only.
  • The app is served as a static web page via GitHub Pages.

Your controls

  • Export: download your cycle log at any time.
  • Delete: delete your account and all associated data at any time; deletion is permanent.
  • Unpair: end a partner pairing at any time, immediately revoking their access.
  • Opt out: turn off status sharing without deleting your log.

Retention

Data is retained while your account is active. Deleted accounts and their data are permanently purged from active systems and backups within 30 days.

Children

This app is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Medical disclaimer

This app provides general wellness information and predictions based on self-reported data. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a healthcare provider for medical concerns.

Payments & subscriptions

If you subscribe to CycleCare Premium, payment is processed by Google Play Billing — we do not receive or store your card number or full payment details. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status to unlock Premium features.

Changes to this policy

We'll notify users in-app of material changes before they take effect.
